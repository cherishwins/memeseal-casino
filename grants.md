All programs verified as active or recently active as of May 2026. Apply in the order listed — fastest cash and best-fit first.

## Tier 1: Apply this week

### 1. Base Builder Grants (Coinbase / Base)

**What:** Retroactive grants of 1-5 ETH for shipped work on Base. Funding is awarded *after* work is live — prioritizing execution over intent.

**Why you qualify:** x402-facilitator is shipped on Base. outlier-clothiers, memeseal-casino, notaryton-bot all interact with Base USDC via the facilitator. You have living code, live deployments, and four consumer apps using it.

**How to apply:**
- Self-nominate via the Base Builder Grants form (link via [docs.base.org/get-started/get-funded](https://docs.base.org/get-started/get-funded))
- Or get nominated by a community member who knows your work
- Or apply via Gitcoin: gitcoin.co/apps/base-builder-grants

**What to submit:**
- x402-facilitator GitHub URL
- Live facilitator URL
- The 4 consumer apps consuming it (outlier-clothiers, memeseal-casino, notaryton-bot, music)
- The one real mainnet tx hash you're about to generate

**Expected award:** 1-5 ETH (~$3,500-$17,500 at current prices). Decision in weeks.

**Critical:** Send that real mainnet tx first. Retroactive grants need shipped, working evidence. A facilitator that has never processed real money is weaker than one that has.

### 2. TON Foundation Grants Program

**What:** Direct grants for Telegram Mini Apps and TON ecosystem projects. Aimed at projects that can "onboard the mass audience of Telegram into TON and web3."

**Why you qualify:** memeseal-casino is a TON Mini App with TON Connect integration. outlier-clothiers fits the social/e-commerce category. Both are deployed.

**How to apply:** ton.org/en/grants — fill out the application form with project description, technical architecture, growth plan, and team background.

**What to emphasize:**
- "Strong social element" — Telegram-native, viral mechanics, referral support
- "Easy onboarding" — demo mode, no wallet needed to evaluate
- "Mass audience activation" — leverages Telegram's distribution

**Expected award:** Variable. Past gaming/GameFi grants have been in the $10K-$100K range.

**Critical:** TON wants apps that grow Telegram. Frame the casino as a Telegram audience-acquisition vehicle, not just a casino.

## Application strategy

**One application per week, not all at once.**

Each application takes 3-4 hours done properly. Doing 8 in one sitting produces 8 mediocre applications. Doing 1 per week, well-researched and customized, produces 8 strong ones.

**Template each application against your strongest asset.**
- Base Builder Grants → emphasize x402-facilitator + apps consuming it
- TON Foundation → emphasize Telegram Mini App distribution
- CDP Grants → emphasize Coinbase developer ecosystem contribution
- Optimism → emphasize Superchain public goods
- x402 Foundation → emphasize ecosystem builder positioning

**Track in a spreadsheet:**
| Program | Submitted | Status | Amount asked | Decision date |
|---|---|---|---|---|

**Don't undersell.** Past grants of $10K-$50K are common. Asking for $25K-$100K is normal for projects with shipped code and live users.

**Show, don't tell.** Every application leads with: live URL, GitHub URL, real tx hash, screenshots of working apps, names of consumer apps consuming the infrastructure. Words come second.

## After grants — the venture path

If 2-3 grants land in the next 90 days, you have proof + capital + credibility to consider:

- **Coinbase Ventures direct outreach** — they invest in x402 ecosystem players
- **Base Ecosystem Fund** — same conversation
- **Crypto-native angels** — Balaji, Naval, etc. have written checks for less

But: don't chase venture capital until you have at least one paying customer. License sales prove demand more than grants do.
