▼ Usage Rules

  • The second word is always italic, always bullion gold. The italic is the signature.
  • Minimum clearspace around the mark = height of the capital T. Nothing intrudes inside.
  • Minimum size for the horizontal lockup: 120px wide on screen, 24mm on print.
  • Minimum size for the monogram: 16px (favicon), 88px (app icon).
  • Approved fields only: Onyx, Carbon, Paper, Bullion, Ember, Bone. Nothing else.
  • Division marks always preceded by "The" — never "House" alone, always "The House."
  • Never recolor the italic. Bullion only (or Onyx on gold/bone fields, Bone on ember).
  • Never use the wordmark over photography without an Onyx or Bullion plate behind it.
  • Never stretch, skew, condense, or apply effects. The mark is monumental, not malleable.
  • Never substitute "Truest Story" for "Truest Story" — the italic carries the brand.
